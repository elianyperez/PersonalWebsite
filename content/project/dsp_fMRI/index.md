## The question

How do navigation strategies relate to brain function across adulthood?

## My contribution

My CV documents research and presentations on the neural correlates of strategy shifts in navigation behavior, alongside my training in spatial cognition and navigational neuroscience.

## The approach

This project brings together behavioral questions about navigation strategy and neural measures. The existing portfolio identifies R and shell scripting as part of the project toolkit.

## What came out of it

The work was shared through presentations at navigation and aging conferences. The original project page provides the supporting materials; detailed findings are not reproduced here because they were not available in the accessible source files.
