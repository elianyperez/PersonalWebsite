## The question

What makes a developer miss a security issue in an API they trust?

## My contribution

I coauthored “API Blindspots: Why experienced developers write vulnerable code,” connecting research on cognition and individual differences with software security.

## The approach

The study involved 109 developers in four countries solving programming puzzles involving Java APIs. The team examined blindspot detection alongside experience, confidence, cognitive measures, and personality.

## What came out of it

Blindspots were associated with poorer identification of security concerns, especially in I/O-related APIs and more complex puzzles. Greater experience or cognitive functioning did not predict better detection; openness was associated with greater detection.
