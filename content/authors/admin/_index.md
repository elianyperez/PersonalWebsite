I’m Eliany, a data analyst and quantitative researcher with a PhD in Behavioral and Cognitive Neuroscience from the University of Florida. I look at how data, tools, and people fit together, from the way information is collected to the decisions it supports.

At TEACH.org, I work across dashboards, data matching, outcome analysis, and reporting workflows. I’m interested in making the whole process easier to understand and maintain, including the definitions, handoffs, and manual steps behind the final report.
