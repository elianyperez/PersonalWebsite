A little about me

Understanding the whole.
Working through the details.

I’m Eliany, a data analyst and quantitative researcher with a PhD in Behavioral and Cognitive Neuroscience from the University of Florida. I’m interested in how people make decisions and how the systems around them support or complicate that process.

At TEACH.org, my work connects data collection, matching, reporting, and the workflows teams use to manage them. A dashboard is one part of that work. The definitions behind a number, the steps needed to produce it, and the way someone uses it all matter too.

I work with Tableau, Alteryx, Python, Power BI, Salesforce, and advanced Excel. I look for where information gets lost, where work is repeated, and where a clearer process or connection between tools could help.
