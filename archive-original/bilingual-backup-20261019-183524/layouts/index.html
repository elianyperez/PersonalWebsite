A little about me

A research mind.
A practical approach.

I’m Eliany, a data analyst and quantitative researcher with a PhD in Behavioral and Cognitive Neuroscience from the University of Florida.

At TEACH.org, I build dashboards, connect data across systems, and analyze outcomes to help teams understand their impact. My research background shapes how I work: ask good questions, pay attention to the details, and make the findings useful.

My toolkit includes Tableau, Alteryx, Python, Power BI, Salesforce, and advanced Excel. The part I enjoy most is helping people make sense of what their data is telling them.
