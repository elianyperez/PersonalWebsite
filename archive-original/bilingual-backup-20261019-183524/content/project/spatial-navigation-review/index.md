## The question

Why do people differ so much in their ability to navigate?

## My contribution

I coauthored “Individual differences in spatial navigation” with Steven M. Weisberg, bringing together research on variation in how people find their way.

## The approach

The review distinguishes navigation outcomes from navigation processes and organizes several levels of explanation for individual variability. It provides a framework for interpreting differences in navigation.

## What came out of it

The chapter offers a structured overview of the research, making a complex area easier to understand and investigate.
